Free music distribution in India: is it actually worth it?

Free distribution is real, but nothing is truly free. Here is what you actually give up, and when that trade is worth it.

By Siby John, thirteen years inside the platforms that decide which music gets seen: Marketing Manager at YouTube India from 2022 to 2025, working with 400+ artists and labels, now Senior Creator Manager at LinkedIn. Reviewed 31 August 2026.
Short answer

Several distributors let you get on Spotify, Apple Music and JioSaavn for free, they make money by taking a percentage of your royalties (often around 10 to 20%) instead of an upfront fee. Free is fine when your streams are low or uncertain. Once you earn steadily, a flat annual fee usually keeps more money in your pocket. Do the math at your real numbers, not the marketing.

How free distribution makes money

A free distributor charges nothing upfront and instead keeps a cut of everything you earn, forever, or until you leave. That is not a scam, it is a revenue-split model. The question is only whether the split costs you more than a flat fee would at your streaming volume.

When free is the right choice

  • You are releasing your first songs and have no idea what they will earn.
  • Your monthly streams are low, so a percentage of a small number is a small number.
  • You want zero financial risk to get started.

For most new artists, starting free is completely sensible. Use our streaming royalty calculator to see roughly what your streams are worth first.

When a flat fee wins

Once a song earns consistently, a percentage cut adds up fast. Run the comparison: if 15% of your annual royalties is more than a flat-fee distributor's yearly price, switch. Frequent releasers almost always come out ahead on flat fee. The full model comparison is in the best music distributor for India.

The non-negotiables, free or paid

  • Your rights stay yours. Free or paid, the distributor takes money, never ownership of your master or publishing.
  • Clean exit. Check you can leave and keep your ISRCs so your streams carry over.
  • India reach. Confirm delivery to JioSaavn and Gaana, and caller-tune support if that channel matters.

People also ask

What is the catch with free music distribution?

The catch is a percentage cut of your royalties instead of an upfront fee. It is a fair trade at low volumes, but it can cost more than a flat fee once you earn steadily.

Can I switch from a free distributor to a paid one later?

Yes, if you migrate carefully and keep the same ISRCs so playlists and stream counts carry over. Never take music down without a migration plan.

Which is cheaper, free or flat-fee distribution?

Whichever is cheaper at your actual streaming numbers. Low or uncertain streams favour free; steady, higher earnings favour a flat annual fee.

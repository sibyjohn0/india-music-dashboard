How much does Spotify pay per stream in India?

The honest answer is that there is no fixed rate, and the per-stream number matters far less than most artists think. Here is how it actually works.

Short answer

There is no fixed per-stream rate. Spotify pools its revenue and splits it by each track's share of total streams, and payouts on streams from India sit well below global averages because revenue per listener is lower here. A stream from India is often worth a small fraction of a rupee. What you earn depends far more on your total streams and where your listeners are than on any single per-stream figure.

Why there is no fixed per-stream rate

Spotify does not pay a set amount per play. It takes the revenue it earns in a market, pools it, and pays each rights holder a share based on that track's percentage of total streams. This is called a stream-share or pro-rata model. Because the size of the pool and the total number of streams both change every month, the effective per-stream rate is never a constant, it is simply the money divided by the plays.

That is why two artists with the same stream count can be paid differently: what matters is where those streams came from and what Spotify earned there.

Why India payouts are lower

Payouts track the revenue a platform makes per listener. In India, subscription prices and advertising rates are much lower than in Western markets, so the revenue pool shared across streams from India is smaller. It is not a penalty aimed at Indian artists, it reflects what the market pays. The practical result is that a stream from a paid listener in the US or UK is worth many times more than a stream from India.

A rough way to estimate your earnings in rupees

Any rupee figure is an estimate, but you can sketch one: multiply your India streams by an approximate per-stream range of a small fraction of a rupee, then subtract your distributor's cut. For example, a track with 100,000 streams that are mostly from India might return a few thousand rupees, not a few lakh. Streams from higher-revenue countries lift that average considerably. Your distributor's dashboard is the only accurate source, treat everything else as a guide.

What actually grows your streaming income

  • Geography mix: building listeners abroad as well as at home raises your average payout per stream.
  • Save and repeat rate: saves and returning listeners feed Spotify's own recommendations, which drive far more streams than any single playlist.
  • A growing catalogue: consistent releases mean more tracks earning at once.
  • Editorial and curator placements: pitched through Spotify for Artists and directly to curators. See our Reviewer Database for curators beyond Spotify.

The honest takeaway

Streaming is a discovery layer, not a salary. Independent income in India is stacked across live shows, sync and licensing, merch, YouTube, and direct fan support, with streaming feeding the top of that funnel. If your streaming income feels small, that is normal, the fix is to build the other layers around it, not to chase the per-stream number.

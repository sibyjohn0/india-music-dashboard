How to get sync placements in India

Sync is often the biggest single cheque an independent artist can earn. Here is how placements actually happen.

By Siby John, thirteen years inside the platforms that decide which music gets seen: Marketing Manager at YouTube India from 2022 to 2025, working with 400+ artists and labels, now Senior Creator Manager at LinkedIn. Reviewed 1 August 2026.
Short answer

Sync is your music licensed into ads, film, series, and games, often the highest single payment available to an independent artist. To win placements you need sync-ready tracks with clean metadata and clear one-stop ownership, tagged by mood and use, sent to the agencies, supervisors, and production houses that place your kind of music, and you must be able to license fast.

What sync is and why it pays

A sync placement is your music used in visual media, an ad, a film, a series, a game, in exchange for a licence fee (and sometimes ongoing royalties). Because a single placement can pay more than months of streaming, sync is one of the most valuable channels an independent artist has.

What makes a track sync-ready

  • Clean metadata: writers and splits, ISRC and ISWC, tempo, and mood.
  • One-stop ownership: clear, undisputed rights so a supervisor can license quickly. Uncleared samples or messy splits kill placements, sort your rights first.
  • Tags by mood and use: so your track surfaces for the right brief.

Who to reach

Music supervisors, sync agents, ad agencies, and production houses place music. Reach the ones who work with your kind of sound. Our Labels and Agencies directory includes agencies and context on who to approach.

How to pitch sync

Send sync-ready tracks tagged for mood and use, with a short note and one-stop ownership stated clearly. Speed matters: supervisors work to deadlines, so friction loses placements. Both existing tracks and bespoke work are routes.

The India angle

Ad films, OTT series, and regional cinema are active sync markets in India. Regional-language and distinctive independent music can stand out for briefs that want something beyond stock.

The takeaway

Make your catalogue sync-ready, keep ownership clean, and build relationships with the people who place music. The Playbook has a sync-ready checklist, the Answers hub covers sync metadata and clearance, and the programme can prep your catalogue and introductions.

People also ask

What are sync deals, and how are they different from royalty-free music?

A sync deal licenses your specific song for a specific visual, an ad, film, or show, for a negotiated fee. Royalty-free libraries sell generic tracks cheaply for any use; a sync places your actual song and usually pays far more.

How do I license a song for a YouTube video or for brands in India?

Brands and creators license music directly from the rights holders, you, or your publisher or label, for a fee and a defined usage. Have a clean split sheet ready and be prepared to grant the recording and composition rights together.

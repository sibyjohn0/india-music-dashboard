How to book live gigs as an independent band in India

Live careers here are built one room at a time, on your own draw and relationships. Here is how to start booking.

By Siby John, thirteen years inside the platforms that decide which music gets seen: Marketing Manager at YouTube India from 2022 to 2025, working with 400+ artists and labels, now Senior Creator Manager at LinkedIn. Reviewed 1 August 2026.
Short answer

Build a tight EPK and live video, target venues and promoters that fit your sound and draw, and start where you can realistically fill a room. A proven local draw is what unlocks bigger stages and better fees. Relationships and consistency matter more than cold pitching, and you do not need a label to begin.

It starts with your draw

Venues and promoters book acts that bring people on the night. Before anything else, be honest about how many you can pull in your own city, and start where you can fill the room. A packed small show does more for your booking future than an empty big one.

What you need before you pitch

  • A tight EPK with your bio, photos, links, and any draw or press.
  • Live video that proves you can hold a room.
  • Dates you can actually play, and a realistic ask.

Who to pitch

Target the venues, promoters, and event curators whose crowd matches your sound, not every stage in the city. Our Labels and Agencies directory lists booking contacts, and the Radar tracks venues and shows across Indian cities.

How to pitch

Keep it short and specific: who you are, your draw, why you fit their night, live video, and dates. Make the promoter's job easy. A focused pitch to the right person beats a mass email every time.

Fees and negotiation

Early shows are often door deals or small guarantees; proper fees come as your draw grows. Know your worth as your audience builds, get the terms in writing, fee, timings, tech, and hospitality, and treat every show as a relationship for the next one.

Climb the ladder

Most acts move through a rough progression: open mics, support slots, your own headline nights, then multi-city runs. Each rung is unlocked by the draw and reputation you built on the last. See how live fits into your income.

The takeaway

Build your draw, prepare a real EPK and live video, pitch the right rooms, and climb steadily. The live chapter of the Playbook covers touring and riders, and the programme works on your live plan and contracts.

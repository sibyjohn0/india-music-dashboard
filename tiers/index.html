02
What artist development is

The through-line that connects every release.

Not a one-off campaign. It's the strategy that makes each release build on the last, instead of starting from zero every time.

Positioning

Your sound, your story, who it's for.

Release strategy

A planned body of work, not scattered singles.

Audience

Reach that converts to followers, then fans.

Foundation

Rights & royalties set up so you keep what you earn.

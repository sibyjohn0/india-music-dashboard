Payment Collection Notes:

  • Collect balance before or at load-in — not after the show.
  • Request TDS certificate (Section 194C, 1% for individuals/HUF, 2% for companies) from corporate promoters within 30 days.
  • Issue GST invoices for fees above INR 20 lakh threshold if registered. Even below threshold, keep all receipts.

Section 8: Post-tour Action Items

  • Update streaming/social analytics baseline

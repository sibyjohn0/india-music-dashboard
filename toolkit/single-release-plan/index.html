4 Weeks Before Release

Production Finalisation

  • Final master audio received (WAV, 24-bit / 44.1kHz)
  • Radio edit prepared (under 3:30, clean)
  • Instrumental version prepared
  • Cover artwork finalised (3000×3000px, JPEG/PNG)
  • ISRC code confirmed via distributor
  • Co-writer split sheet signed (if applicable)
  • IPRS work registration filed
  • PPL India recording registered

Distribution

  • Uploaded to distributor (DistroKid / TuneCore / Believe / OKListen)
  • All metadata entered correctly (title, artist name, composer, lyricist, producer)
  • Release date confirmed in distributor dashboard
  • Pre-save link generated

Editorial Pitch

  • Spotify editorial pitch submitted (7+ days minimum before release — do this NOW for 4-weeks-out timing)
  • Apple Music for Artists editorial pitch submitted
  • JioSaavn editorial pitch (via distributor or direct contact)

3 Weeks Before Release

Press

  • Press release written and finalised
  • Press materials ready: bio, artwork, stream link (private until embargo date)
  • Press list compiled (target 5–10 outlets based on genre fit):
  • Wild City
  • Homegrown
  • Rolling Stone India
  • The Hindu
  • Scroll.in
  • City-specific press (Bengaluru Mirror, Mumbai Mirror, etc.)
  • Relevant podcasts and YouTube music channels
  • Press outreach sent to all targets with embargo date set

Social Media

  • Content calendar for 4-week campaign created
  • Teaser clips / snippets created (15-sec, 30-sec, 60-sec)
  • Instagram Reel snippet scheduled (1 per week minimum)
  • Announcement post prepared (release date reveal)

2 Weeks Before Release

  • Announcement post published: release date revealed across all platforms
  • Pre-save campaign promoted heavily on social media
  • Email/WhatsApp list: pre-release announcement sent
  • Music video (if any): teaser trailer posted on YouTube / Instagram
  • Behind-the-scenes content released (studio photos, making-of)
  • Playlist curator pitching campaign started:
  • SubmitHub submissions sent
  • Groover credits used for key curators
  • Direct DMs to 5–10 specific curators on Spotify / Instagram
  • Radio plugger briefed (if using one) or direct radio station outreach sent
  • Guest blog / feature article published (if media partnership arranged)
  • Podcast interview recorded or scheduled

Release Day

  • Check single is live on all platforms: Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk, YouTube Music, Amazon Music
  • Instagram main post live (artwork + caption + Spotify link in bio)
  • Instagram Reel live (best snippet)
  • Instagram Stories: share, polls ("have you listened?"), countdown complete
  • YouTube: music video live (if applicable) or visualiser posted
  • Facebook event or post live
  • Twitter/X post live
  • Email/WhatsApp to mailing list sent
  • Spotify for Artists pre-save link converts — check new followers and streams
  • Respond to every comment and share in the first 6 hours

First Week Post-release

  • Monitor Spotify for Artists daily: streams, saves, listener geography
  • Share any press coverage as it goes live
  • Post fan reposts and reaction content
  • New content: acoustic version snippet, lyric video, live performance clip
  • Follow up with press who haven't published yet (once, politely)
  • Check if algorithmic playlists have picked up the track (Discover Weekly, Release Radar)
  • Share milestone posts: "1,000 streams in 24 hours" (adapt to reality)

Weeks 2–4 Post-release

  • Continue posting related content (2–3 times per week)
  • Live performance of the single released (if not yet)
  • Playlist curator follow-up (politely check on pitches sent)
  • Instagram paid boost of best-performing organic post (₹1,000–5,000 test budget)
  • YouTube ad campaign for music video (₹2,000–10,000 if applicable)
  • Radio airplay check-in (if radio campaign is active)
  • Consider: acoustic version, remix, or cover response to extend song's life

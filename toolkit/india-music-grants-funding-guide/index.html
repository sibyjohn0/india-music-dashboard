India Music Grants & Funding Guide

A free, practical guide for independent Indian artists. Part of the Indie Music India toolkit.

India Artist Management Toolkit — Money Kit

Grants are non-dilutive funding — you do not give up rights, royalties, or equity. Most Indian indie artists are unaware of what is available. This guide covers every credible grant, fellowship, residency, and scheme an independent musician based in India can apply for.

How to Use This Guide

1. Read through every category and mark which ones you are eligible for. 2. Note the application windows — most open once a year. 3. Prepare your artist portfolio (bio, recordings, press, project proposal) so you can apply quickly when windows open. 4. Apply to multiple schemes simultaneously — there is no conflict between receiving grants from different bodies. 5. Track applications in the Application Tracker at the end of this document.

1. Government of India — Central Schemes

Sangeet Natak Akademi (Sna)

Website: sangeetnatak.gov.in India's national academy of music, dance, and drama, under the Ministry of Culture.

SNA Fellowships (Akademi Ratna / Akademi Puraskar):

  • For outstanding contribution to Indian performing arts — competitive, prestigious
  • Not application-based; nominations accepted from the field

SNA Financial Assistance Scheme:

  • Grants to cultural organisations and individuals for specific projects
  • Covers workshops, recordings, performances, documentation
  • Amounts: typically ₹1–5 lakh per project
  • Apply via: sangeetnatak.gov.in/schemes

SNA Cultural Troupes Scheme:

  • Supports touring and cultural exchange programmes
  • Relevant for artists wanting to perform in underserved regions or internationally

Iccr — Indian Council for Cultural Relations

Website: iccr.gov.in

Artist Grants (Cultural Exchange):

  • Funds Indian artists to participate in international cultural programmes
  • Apply when invited by a foreign institution with ICCR backing
  • Covers travel, accommodation, performance fees

Empanelment for International Tours:

  • Get onto the ICCR empanelled artist list to be considered for state-sponsored international tours
  • Apply via: iccr.gov.in/artist-empanelment

MINISTRY OF CULTURE — ZONAL CULTURAL CENTRES (ZCCs)

Seven ZCCs across India, each running grant and development schemes for regional artists.

  • North Zone Cultural Centre (Patiala)
  • West Zone Cultural Centre (Udaipur)
  • South Zone Cultural Centre (Thanjavur)
  • East Zone Cultural Centre (Kolkata)
  • North East Zone Cultural Centre (Dimapur)
  • North Central Cultural Centre (Prayagraj)
  • South Central Cultural Centre (Nagpur)

Each ZCC runs its own annual schemes. Check your regional ZCC website for current calls.

Scheme for Young Artists (Sya)

  • Ministry of Culture scheme supporting young performing artists
  • Monthly stipend for 2 years while pursuing advanced training
  • Age limit: typically 18–30
  • Apply via: Ministry of Culture portal (indiaculture.nic.in)

2. State Government Schemes

Most state governments run their own arts and culture departments with annual grants. Key ones:

Maharashtra: Maharashtra Rajya Sahitya ani Sanskriti Mandal — grants for performing arts projects Karnataka: Department of Kannada and Culture — Rajyotsava awards, project grants Tamil Nadu: Tamil Nadu Arasu Nataka Mandram — performance and creation grants Delhi: Delhi Arts Commission — project grants, residencies for Delhi-based artists Kerala: Kerala Sangeetha Nataka Akademi — classical and folk music focus West Bengal: Paschim Banga Sangeet Akademi

How to find your state scheme: Search "[your state] arts council grants" or contact the state's Department of Cultural Affairs directly.

3. International Cultural Organisations — India Offices

British Council India

Website: britishcouncil.in

  • Artist grants for India-UK cultural collaboration projects
  • UK/India Artist Exchange residencies
  • Music collaboration grants (when thematic calls are open)
  • Connect with: Arts team at britishcouncil.in/arts

Goethe Institut — Max Mueller Bhavan (India)

Website: goethe.de/india

  • Germany-India cultural collaboration grants
  • Residencies at German cultural institutions for Indian artists
  • Fund-It grants for collaborative projects with German partners

Alliance Francaise (India)

  • France-India music and cultural collaboration funding
  • Residencies in France for Indian artists
  • Connect via: ambafrance-in.org or local Alliance Francaise office

US Embassy — American Center India

  • Fulbright fellowships for advanced study or research in the US (includes music)
  • Cultural exchange grants for India-US collaborative projects

PRO Helvetia — Swiss Arts Council

  • Grants for Switzerland-India cultural collaboration
  • Residencies in Switzerland
  • Website: prohelvetia.in

4. Independent and Private Foundations

Itc Sangeet Research Academy (Sra)

  • Scholarships and fellowships for Hindustani classical music
  • Prestigious; residential programme in Kolkata
  • Website: itcsra.org

Shriram Bharatiya Kala Kendra

  • New Delhi-based institution with grants and residencies for classical arts

Spic Macay

  • Not grants but significant performance opportunities for classical and folk artists
  • Pan-India network of college chapters; good for exposure and modest performance fees

Tata Trusts — Cultural Grants

  • Tata Trusts fund cultural preservation and innovation projects
  • Apply via: tatatrusts.org/our-work/arts-culture

Csr-funded Arts Grants

Many large Indian companies allocate CSR funding for arts and culture. Approach:

  • Infosys Foundation
  • HCL Foundation
  • Wipro Foundation
  • Godrej India Culture Lab (Mumbai)
  • India Foundation for the Arts (IFA) — ifa.org.in

Ifa (India Foundation for the Arts)

  • Arts Research grant
  • Arts Practice grant
  • Arts Education grant
  • Amounts: ₹1–10 lakh depending on category
  • Website: ifa.org.in/programmes/grants

5. International Music Residencies Open to Indian Artists

  • IRCAM (Paris) — electronic and experimental music residencies
  • Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity (Canada) — music residencies, open application
  • Red Bull Music Academy (when active) — global; previously had Indian participants
  • Berklee India Exchange (via Berklee College of Music) — workshops, not full residencies

6. Crowdfunding and Community Funding (Not Grants but Worth Tracking)

India-specific:

  • Ketto (ketto.org) — project-based crowdfunding
  • Wishberry (wishberry.in) — India's dedicated creative crowdfunding platform
  • Fuel A Dream (fueladream.com) — creative project funding

International:

  • Kickstarter — available to Indian creators
  • Indiegogo — available to Indian creators
  • Bandcamp crowdfunding — through fan subscriptions

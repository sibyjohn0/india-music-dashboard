Music Business Plan Template, India

Instructions: Complete one plan per artist at the start of the management relationship. Review and update every 6 months. This is a working document, not a pitch deck.

Section 1: the Artist

1.1 Who are they? [Write 2-3 sentences describing the artist as a musical entity. What is the sound? What is the emotional territory? Who does it remind people of, and how is it different?]

1.2 What stage are they at?

  • Pre-release (no public music yet)
  • Early (1-3 releases, under 5,000 monthly listeners)
  • Growing (4+ releases, 5,000-50,000 monthly listeners)
  • Established (50,000+ monthly listeners, regular live income)

1.3 What is genuinely good about this artist? [Not marketing language. What is the real reason someone would seek this artist out over everything else available?]

1.4 What is the honest challenge? [What is currently holding this artist back? Be specific.]

Section 2: the Market

2.1 Indian Indie Landscape India has approximately 400-500 actively releasing independent artists across genres. The domestic streaming market is enormous but per-stream rates are low (approximately USD 0.001-0.003 per stream on major platforms from Indian listeners vs. USD 0.003-0.005 from US/UK listeners). The live market in Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad) is the most reliable revenue source for independent artists earning below 1M monthly listeners.

2.2 Target Audience Primary audience: [Describe the most likely fan. Age range, cities, what else they listen to, how they discover music] Secondary audience: [Who else might find this artist?]

2.3 Comparable Artists (Comps) List 3-5 artists whose career trajectory you are using as a reference point:

[e.g. Prateek Kuhad: Monthly Listeners: 2.5M Live fee range: INR 3-8L per show Label/Independent: Independent What it tells us: Shows what's possible without a label deal]

2.4 Opportunity [What is the specific opening in the market this artist can occupy? Is there a genre, language, city, or audience that is underserved?]

Section 3: Goals

3.1 12-Month Goals (be specific and measurable)

3.2 3-Year Vision [What does success look like in 3 years? Describe it concretely, what the artist is releasing, where they are performing, what their income is, who knows their name.]

Section 4: Revenue Model

4.3 Priority Revenue Focus Given the artist's current stage, which 1-2 revenue streams should be the primary focus in the next 12 months, and why? [e.g. "Live performances in Bengaluru and Pune, because the artist already has a following in these cities and has the stage presence to convert shows into word-of-mouth growth."]

Startup Expenses Template

A free, ready-to-use template for independent Indian artists. Read it here, or make your own copy to fill in.

This template is for artists who are formally launching their music career as a business, tracking all one-time setup costs before regular monthly operations begin. Knowing your startup costs upfront helps you plan how much capital you need to raise or save before you go full-time.

How to Use This Template

1. List every one-time cost needed to launch your music business. 2. Separate Must-Haves from Nice-to-Haves. 3. Total the Must-Haves, this is your minimum viable launch budget. 4. Compare to your available capital (see Opening Balance template). 5. If there is a gap, identify what to defer or how to fund it.

How to Fund the Gap

1. Defer nice-to-have items to Year 2, release with minimum viable setup 2. Apply for grants (see India Music Grants & Funding Guide) 3. Crowdfunding for first release (Wishberry, Ketto, Indiegogo) 4. Offer pre-orders or fan pledges via Bandcamp 5. Negotiate session or teaching work to fund recording costs 6. Udyam-registered businesses can access priority MSME loans from nationalised banks 7. MUDRA Loan (PM SVANidhi): up to ₹10 lakh for micro-enterprises, artists qualify as creative freelancers

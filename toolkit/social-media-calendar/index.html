Instagram

Best post times for Indian audience: 7–9 AM, 12–2 PM, 7–10 PM IST Feed Posts: 3–4 per week (high quality, evergreen) Reels: 5–7 per week during active campaign (discovery tool) Stories: Daily (lower production, interactive, ephemeral) Goal: _________ new followers this month

Youtube

Best upload times: Wednesday–Friday, 4–8 PM IST Videos: 1–2 per week during campaign (music video, live session, vlog) Shorts: 3–5 per week (repurposed from Reels or standalone) Goal: _________ new subscribers this month

Twitter / X

Best times: 8–10 AM, 12–2 PM, 7–9 PM IST Posts: 2–3 per day (mix of announcements, replies, retweets) Goal: Engagement and community interaction

Monthly Content Checklist

Reels / Short Video

  • Studio/rehearsal snippet
  • Song snippet (30-sec of latest release)
  • "Day in the life" clip
  • Transition / hook content using trending audio
  • Behind-the-scenes of show or recording session

Instagram Feed

  • Artist portrait / live photo (professional quality)
  • Artwork post for latest release
  • Gig announcement graphic
  • Milestone post (streaming, follower count)
  • Throwback / archive shot

Stories

  • Polls ("Which song should we release next?")
  • Q&A box
  • Countdown sticker for release or show
  • Share fan posts / reposts
  • Daily check-in: what are you working on

Youtube

  • Music video (if releasing)
  • Acoustic / stripped version
  • Live performance recording
  • Artist vlog (brief, authentic)
  • Shorts from above content

India-specific Content Opportunities

Seasonal

  • Diwali / Navratri / Holi: Festive creative tie-ins (relevant for Hindi-language acts especially)
  • Independence Day (15 Aug) and Republic Day (26 Jan): Patriotic or reflective content
  • Monsoon season (Jun–Sep): Visual and mood content themes
  • New Year (Jan 1 and Diwali calendar): Reflection and release planning posts

TRENDING AUDIO (Instagram / YouTube Shorts): Use trending audio overlays on Reels — even if the audio is not yours. This expands algorithmic reach significantly in India. Pair trending audio with visual content about your music, tour, or process.

Collaborations

Collaborate with other Indian indie artists on content (co-Reels, joint Stories, shared playlists). Cross-pollination between artist audiences is one of the most efficient growth tactics in the Indian indie space.

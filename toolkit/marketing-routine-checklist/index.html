Marketing Routine Checklist

A free, practical guide for independent Indian artists. Part of the Indie Music India toolkit.

A structured set of daily, weekly, and monthly marketing tasks for an independent artist in India. Consistency in these routines compounds over time.

DAILY TASKS (10–20 minutes)

Social Media

  • ✓Check Instagram notifications: reply to comments on recent posts (within 24 hours)
  • ✓Check Instagram DMs: respond to fan messages and industry contacts (within 24 hours)
  • ✓Post 1 Instagram Story (can be informal: studio update, what you're listening to, a question)
  • ✓Engage genuinely with 5–10 posts from artists you respect, curators, media outlets, or fans (not just likes — meaningful comments)
  • ✓Check and respond to YouTube comments on recent videos
  • ✓Check Twitter/X mentions and respond if warranted

STREAMING MONITORING (during active release only)

  • ✓Spot-check Spotify for Artists: streams, listener trend
  • ✓Note any new playlist adds

WEEKLY TASKS (1–2 hours per week)

Content

  • ✓Post 3–4 high-quality Instagram feed posts or Reels (per social media calendar)
  • ✓Upload 1 YouTube video or Short (or schedule next video in queue)
  • ✓Post 2–3 Twitter/X posts (announcements, reactions, industry conversation)
  • ✓Draft captions for next week's scheduled posts

Press and Media

  • ✓Check for any new press mentions (Google Alert for your artist name, set at: google.com/alerts)
  • ✓Share any new press coverage on social media and tag the publication
  • ✓If in active press campaign: follow up on any outstanding press submissions

INDUSTRY OUTREACH (as applicable)

  • ✓Send 2–3 targeted playlist curator pitches (SubmitHub / Groover / direct DM)
  • ✓Follow up on any pending booking or PR conversations
  • ✓Check for upcoming festival or venue submission deadlines

Data Review

  • ✓Review Instagram Insights for past week: what content performed best?
  • ✓Check Spotify for Artists weekly summary
  • ✓Note any changes in listener geography that affect touring or advertising plans

MONTHLY TASKS (2–4 hours per month)

Analytics

  • ✓Complete Artist Audience Report (see Artist Audience Report Template)
  • ✓Pull Instagram Insights export for the month
  • ✓Pull YouTube Studio monthly summary
  • ✓Check distributor royalty dashboard for new statements or payment notifications
  • ✓Check IPRS portal for royalty accruals
  • ✓Check PPL India portal for royalty accruals
  • ✓Update Chartmetric monitoring: any new notable playlist adds?

Content Planning

  • ✓Plan next month's social media calendar (see Social Media Calendar template)
  • ✓Brief any collaborators (photographer, videographer, graphic designer) on upcoming needs
  • ✓Review content performance: which pillars are resonating? What should be increased or cut?

Press and Media

  • ✓Identify 3–5 new press/media targets and add to outreach list
  • ✓If no new press this month, consider: what is the next pitchable story?
  • ✓Update artist bio and one-sheet if anything significant has happened (new show, new release, new streaming milestone)

Industry Development

  • ✓Review the upcoming festival calendar and identify submission deadlines
  • ✓Identify 2–3 new venues, playlists, or brands to introduce yourself to next month
  • ✓Check on open applications: festival slots, grant opportunities (see India Music Grants & Funding Guide)

Financial

  • ✓Reconcile this month's marketing spend against budget
  • ✓Record any new streaming or royalty income received
  • ✓Note invoice and payment status for any outstanding PR, design, or production bills

QUARTERLY TASKS (1–2 days per quarter)

  • ✓Complete full Artist Audience Report for the quarter
  • ✓Marketing campaign post-mortem: what worked, what didn't, what changes for next quarter
  • ✓Update one-sheet with current numbers
  • ✓Review distribution platform performance: are you on the right platform for your needs?
  • ✓Review PR relationship: is your current publicist / PR approach working?
  • ✓Assess brand partnership opportunities: is there a brand alignment worth pursuing?
  • ✓Plan next major release cycle: is the next release ready? When should campaign begin?
  • ✓Review mailing list health: unsubscribe rate, open rate, list size

RELEASE WEEK SPECIFIC TASKS (see Single Release Plan and Album Release Plan Timeline for full detail)

Days Before Release

  • ✓Final check: release live on distributor? Pre-save link working?
  • ✓All social content scheduled and ready
  • ✓Email/WhatsApp list notification ready to send
  • ✓Press embargo confirmed with all media

Release Day

  • ✓Confirm all platforms have release live by 6 AM IST
  • ✓Send email/WhatsApp at 8 AM
  • ✓Post Instagram main post, Reel, and Stories
  • ✓YouTube premiere or video post live
  • ✓Twitter/X announcement
  • ✓Active on all platforms all day: respond, engage, celebrate

POST-RELEASE (Days 1–7):

  • ✓Monitor streams daily
  • ✓Share all press coverage as it goes live
  • ✓Post fan reactions and UGC
  • ✓One new piece of related content (acoustic snippet, live clip, behind-scenes)

A Note on Sustainability

This checklist represents the full marketing routine. Not every independent artist can execute every task every day without burning out. Prioritise as follows:

MINIMUM VIABLE ROUTINE (if time is very limited):

  • ✓Daily: 1 Instagram Story + respond to comments
  • ✓Weekly: 2 feed posts + 1 YouTube Short
  • ✓Monthly: Review data + plan next month

Active Release Routine

Add the full release-week protocol and increase daily social activity for the 4 weeks around any release.

As your team grows (manager, social media help, PR), delegate the routine tasks. Your time as the artist is best spent creating.

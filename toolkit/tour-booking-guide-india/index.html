NOTE ON PRICING: Common price points that work at Indian indie shows:

  • Stickers: ₹50–100
  • Tote bags: ₹300–400
  • Posters: ₹200–300
  • T-Shirts: ₹500–800
  • Hoodies: ₹1,000–1,500
  • Vinyl (if applicable): ₹800–1,200
  • Bundles: Offer a 10–15% saving to encourage upsells

Section 6: Merch Notes & Best Practices

Payment Methods to Accept at Shows

  • Cash: Keep a float of small denomination notes (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100) for change.
  • UPI: Display a QR code on the merch table (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Use a dedicated UPI ID for merch sales so revenue is easy to track.
  • Card (optional): If you have a card reader (e.g., Pine Labs portable POS), use it. Many young concert attendees in India prefer UPI or card over cash.

Venue Commission

  • Most venues in India charge 10–20% of gross merch sales as commission.
  • Negotiate this upfront. Some venues will waive commission for well-known artists.
  • Always confirm in writing whether commission applies before the show.

GST on Merch

  • If you are GST-registered, merch sales are subject to GST. T-shirts and apparel: 12% GST. CDs/physical music media: 12% GST. Check the latest GST schedule at cbic.gov.in.
  • If you are not GST-registered and annual turnover is below INR 20 lakh (or 10 lakh for special category states), GST registration is not mandatory.

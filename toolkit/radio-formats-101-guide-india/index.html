Radio Formats 101 Guide (India)

A free, practical guide for independent Indian artists. Part of the Indie Music India toolkit.

Understanding the different radio formats in India and how your music fits into each.

What Is a Radio Format?

A radio format is the programming identity of a station — it defines what music the station plays, what demographic it targets, and how its shows are structured. Understanding formats is the first step to knowing which stations to target and how to position your pitch.

Major Radio Formats in India

1. Bollywood / Hindi Contemporary Hit Radio (Chr)

The dominant format in India by sheer number of stations and listener numbers.

  • Content: Current and recent Bollywood film songs, Hindi pop, mainstream commercial music
  • Target demographic: 18–45, all income segments
  • Key stations: Radio Mirchi (primary), Big FM, Fever FM
  • Relevance for indie: Very low for English-language indie; moderate for Hindi indie pop and contemporary singer-songwriter acts that can cross over into mainstream Hindi consumption

2. Hindi Retro / Golden Era

  • Content: Classic Hindi film songs from the 1950s–1990s
  • Target: 40+ listeners nostalgic for golden era Bollywood
  • Key stations: AIR Vividh Bharati, Radio City Gold, Mirchi Classic
  • Relevance for indie: None unless you are specifically working in this space

3. ENGLISH CONTEMPORARY (Adult Contemporary / Top 40)

  • Content: International English-language pop, adult contemporary, crossover English acts from India
  • Target: English-speaking urban professionals, 22–40
  • Key stations: Limited — Red FM (some), Radio City (some shows), English-language podcast streams
  • Relevance for indie: The most viable format for English-language Indian indie, but stations are few and playlist rotation is limited

4. Youth / Alternative

  • Content: Alternative, indie, some hip-hop, youth-oriented pop
  • Target: 16–28, metro college-going audience
  • Key stations: Red FM (certain shows, certain cities), specific segments on Radio City
  • Relevance for indie: The primary target for alternative/indie acts. These shows are programmed by music directors who are personally engaged with music discovery.

5. Regional Language Format

  • Content: Regional language music (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi, Malayalam, etc.)
  • Target: Language-community specific listeners across metros and regional cities
  • Key stations: AIR regional channels, private FM stations in specific states (Suryan FM Tamil Nadu, Radio Mango Kerala, Rainbow FM Karnataka, etc.)
  • Relevance for indie: HIGH for acts creating music in regional languages. Regional radio actively programmes independent music in those languages.

6. All India Radio (Air)

AIR operates multiple formats:

  • Vividh Bharati: Entertainment format (Hindi film music, drama)
  • FM Gold (Delhi, Mumbai): Bilingual (English/Hindi) music, news, and features. More open to independent and non-Bollywood music than private FM.
  • FM Rainbow: Regional language formats
  • National Channel: Classical, traditional, and folk music — relevant for classical and folk artists
  • Relevance for indie: AIR FM Gold and FM Rainbow are the most accessible AIR channels for independent artists. AIR has a system for registering as a "B Grade" or "A Grade" artiste which is required for paid performances on AIR. Visit allindiaradio.gov.in for details.

How Radio Programming Decisions Are Made in India

Music Director Rotation

At each station (or city-level station), a Music Director (MD) or Head of Music decides which tracks enter the playlist and how frequently they are rotated.

  • Heavy rotation: Played 15–25 times per day
  • Medium rotation: 8–14 times per day
  • Light rotation: 3–7 times per day
  • Power play: Feature slot, often 1–2 times per day at a specific time

Audience Research

Large stations use research panels and digital feedback to measure which songs their specific city audience likes. Songs that test well stay in rotation longer.

Label Relationships

Major labels have dedicated label managers who maintain ongoing relationships with station music directors. This is why major label releases almost automatically receive consideration — there is a pre-existing relationship. Independent artists must substitute relationship-building and targeted outreach for this structural advantage.

Air Artiste Registration

All India Radio requires artists to register as an "auditioned artiste" before they can receive performance fees for broadcast on AIR. This is separate from commercial FM.

Grade A: Established artists — higher fee Grade B: Mid-level artists — standard fee Grade C: New, emerging artists

Registration process: Contact your nearest AIR station's Audition Committee. Bring recordings and be prepared to perform live in an audition. Grade is assigned by the committee.

AIR fees for broadcast use (indicative, per broadcast): Grade A: INR 15,000+ Grade B: INR 5,000–15,000 Grade C: INR 2,000–5,000

Digital Radio and Streaming Radio in India

Spotify Radio / Apple Music Radio: Generated automatically by DSP algorithms; not programmable directly. Amazon Music Stations: Similar algorithmic approach. Gaana Radio / JioSaavn Radio: Streaming radio features within the apps; artists can claim profiles to improve algorithmic placement. Online radio stations (non-broadcast): A growing number of online-only stations and DJ/curation streams on platforms like Mixcloud are relevant for electronic, ambient, and alternative artists.

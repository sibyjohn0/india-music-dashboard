Shipping Cost Matrix Sheet

A free, ready-to-use template for independent Indian artists. Read it here, or make your own copy to fill in.

A reference sheet for estimating physical product (merch, CDs, vinyl, cassettes) shipping costs within India and internationally. Use this when planning merch pre-orders, store fulfilment, or physical release campaigns.

Section 5: E-commerce & Fulfilment Options (India)

SELF-FULFILMENT (best for small volumes): Pack and ship yourself. Use India Post for low-cost domestic shipping. Tools: Shiprocket, Delhivery API (integrates with your store for automated label printing).

THIRD-PARTY LOGISTICS (3PL) (for larger volumes): Companies like Shiprocket, Pickrr, or Ecom Express offer warehousing and fulfilment. Useful if you are shipping more than 50-100 orders per month.

International Merch Stores

For global fans, consider a separate store via Bandcamp (handles international shipping manually), or Printful/Printify (print-on-demand, no inventory needed, but lower margins). Note: Bandcamp takes 15% on physical items but is widely trusted by international music fans.

Section 6: GST on Shipping (India)

GST is applicable on courier and shipping services:

  • Courier services (private): 18% GST on shipping charges
  • India Post (government): GST-exempt
  • Collect actual shipping cost from the customer
  • If GST-registered, you may claim input tax credit on shipping GST paid

Tax Notes

GST: Services received from Indian studios, engineers, producers, and designers attract 18% GST on the invoice amount if the vendor is GST-registered. Budget for this on top of quoted fees.

TDS: If you pay an individual session musician, engineer, or producer more than INR 30,000 per engagement, TDS at 1% must be deducted (individuals) under Section 194C.

Exports (international services like mixing/mastering): No GST applies on services received from outside India (import of services), but you may need to declare under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) depending on your GST registration status. Consult your CA.

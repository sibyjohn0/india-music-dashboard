Revenue Streams Checklist

A free, practical guide for independent Indian artists. Part of the Indie Music India toolkit.

India Artist Management Toolkit — Money Kit

Use this to audit how many income sources you have activated and identify which ones you are leaving on the table. The goal is not to chase all of them at once — it is to know which levers exist and make deliberate choices about which to build next.

How to Use This Checklist

For each stream: mark Active, Inactive, or Not Applicable. For Active streams: note the approximate monthly income. Review this every quarter and pick one Inactive stream to activate.

Artist On-Boarding Checklist

Artist On-boarding Checklist (India)

Use this checklist when taking on a new artist. Complete all sections within the first 30 days of the management relationship. Items marked * are legally or financially critical.

  • ✓

Section 1: Artist Identity and Biography

  • ✓Legal full name (as per Aadhaar / PAN)
  • ✓Stage name / artist name
  • ✓Date of birth
  • ✓City of residence and home state
  • ✓Contact number and preferred communication channel (WhatsApp / Email)
  • ✓Emergency contact name and number
  • ✓Short bio (150 words) — confirm artist has approved this version
  • ✓Long bio (500 words) — confirm artist has approved this version
  • ✓Artist photo: minimum 3 high-res images (3000px+), cleared for press use
  • ✓Genre(s) — primary and secondary
  • ✓Languages the artist performs in
  • ✓Artist's self-described influences (for press and pitch use)
  • ✓

Section 2: Legal and Financial Documents*

  • ✓PAN Card copy — required for all income tax compliance and TDS
  • ✓Aadhaar Card copy — required for identity verification
  • ✓Bank account details: Account name, account number, IFSC code, bank branch
  • ✓GST registration number (if applicable — required if annual income exceeds INR 20L threshold)
  • ✓Cancelled cheque or bank statement for account verification
  • ✓Signed Artist Management Agreement (dated, witnessed)
  • ✓Confirm whether artist has an existing accountant / CA — if yes, get contact details
  • ✓

Section 3: Existing Contracts and Commitments*

  • ✓Any active record label agreement — get full copy
  • ✓Any active publishing agreement — get full copy
  • ✓Any active distribution agreement (DistroKid, TuneCore, Believe, etc.) — confirm term and exclusivity
  • ✓Any active booking agency agreement — get full copy
  • ✓Any brand endorsement or sponsorship agreements — get full copy
  • ✓Any outstanding advances received (label, publisher, brand) — document amounts and recoupment status
  • ✓Confirmed upcoming shows or tours — dates, venues, fees agreed
  • ✓Any existing copyright registrations (IPRS, Copyright Office) — document
  • ✓

Section 4: Digital Presence Audit

  • ✓Spotify for Artists — confirm artist has claimed profile, note monthly listeners
  • ✓Apple Music for Artists — confirmed claimed
  • ✓YouTube channel — URL, subscriber count, monetisation status (yes/no)
  • ✓Instagram — handle, follower count, average engagement rate
  • ✓Facebook — page URL, follower count
  • ✓Twitter/X — handle, follower count
  • ✓JioSaavn — profile claimed (yes/no)
  • ✓Gaana — profile claimed (yes/no)
  • ✓Wynk — profile claimed (yes/no)
  • ✓Linktree or equivalent smart link — URL
  • ✓Artist website — URL (if exists)
  • ✓Email mailing list — platform used, number of subscribers
  • ✓

Section 5: Existing Music Catalogue

  • ✓Full list of released tracks — title, release date, ISRC code, distributor
  • ✓Full list of unreleased recorded tracks
  • ✓Any music videos released — YouTube URLs
  • ✓Any music licensed for sync (films, ads, TV) — document deals
  • ✓Are masters owned by artist or a label? — confirm for each release
  • ✓Are publishing rights registered with IPRS? — confirm
  • ✓Does artist have a registered performing rights society membership? — IPRS / PPL
  • ✓

Section 6: Live Performance History

  • ✓List of cities performed in (last 12 months)
  • ✓List of key venues performed at
  • ✓Typical fee range for headline shows
  • ✓Typical fee range for festival appearances
  • ✓Has artist performed at NH7 Weekender, Bacardi NH7, Ziro, Magnetic Fields, or equivalent? (key credibility markers)
  • ✓Does artist have a technical rider? — if yes, get copy
  • ✓Does artist have a hospitality rider? — if yes, get copy
  • ✓Stage plot — get current version
  • ✓Input list — get current version
  • ✓Does artist travel with a band? How many members?
  • ✓

Section 7: Press and Media

  • ✓Existing press coverage — collect all published articles and reviews
  • ✓Existing radio play — note stations, show names
  • ✓Any existing PR agency relationship? — if yes, term and exclusivity
  • ✓EPK (Electronic Press Kit) — does one exist? If yes, get current version
  • ✓One-sheet — does one exist? If yes, get current version
  • ✓

Section 8: Goals and Vision (Document in Writing)

  • ✓Artist's 12-month goal (specific and measurable)
  • ✓Artist's 3-year vision
  • ✓Priority: Live performance / Recording / Brand partnerships / Sync / Other?
  • ✓Markets the artist wants to develop (cities, international?)
  • ✓Non-negotiables: things the artist will NOT do (brands, genres, collaborators)
  • ✓Ideal collaborators the artist wants to work with
  • ✓Monthly time available for music career vs. other commitments
  • ✓

Section 9: Financial Baseline

  • ✓Current monthly income from music (all sources combined)
  • ✓Current monthly expenses attributable to music career
  • ✓How is the artist currently funding their music? (savings, day job, family, brand deals)
  • ✓Does artist have a separate bank account for music income? (recommended — set up if not)
  • ✓Is artist filing annual IT returns? — confirm
  • ✓

Section 10: First 30-Day Action Plan

  • ✓Management Agreement signed and filed
  • ✓Intro call completed — goals documented
  • ✓Full digital audit completed
  • ✓All existing contracts reviewed
  • ✓IPRS / PPL registration confirmed or initiated
  • ✓Bank account for music income opened (if not already)
  • ✓Immediate opportunities identified and communicated to artist
  • ✓Monthly check-in schedule agreed
  • ✓

One Sheet Example

A free, ready-to-use template for independent Indian artists. Read it here, or make your own copy to fill in.

A one-sheet is a single-page artist overview document used in pitching to venues, festivals, brands, booking agents, press, and industry contacts. This is a template and example, customise fully for your artist.

What Is a One-sheet

A one-sheet (also called a one-pager or sell sheet) is a concise, professionally formatted document that summarises everything a booking agent, promoter, festival programmer, or press contact needs to know about an artist in a single page. It is the industry standard for artist introductions.

  • Load instantly (PDF format, under 5MB)
  • Be readable in 30 seconds
  • Contain all essential information with no unnecessary filler
  • Look like it belongs in the same room as the professional it's being sent to

One-sheet Creation Guidelines

Design

  • One page only. Never two pages.
  • Use a consistent font system (headline font + body font, no more than 2 fonts)
  • White space is your friend. Overcrowded one-sheets are ignored.
  • Artist photo should be the dominant visual element
  • Your contact information must be easy to find at the bottom

Format

  • Export as PDF. Always.
  • File size: under 5MB. Optimise images before exporting.
  • Do not use a format that requires a specific software to open

Content Rules

  • Every data point (streaming numbers, press quotes, festival credits) must be current and accurate.
  • Update your one-sheet before every major pitch, booking agents and festival programmers notice stale numbers.
  • Do not pad the "select live history" section with shows that don't belong. Two genuinely impressive credits outweigh ten forgettable ones.
  • The bio must be written about you, not by you. Have someone else write it, or at minimum have someone else edit it.

Tools to Build your One-sheet

  • Canva: Free templates, easy layout, exports to PDF
  • Adobe InDesign: Professional tool if you have design skills
  • Adobe Express: Mid-level, free plan available
  • Figma: Good for collaborative design
  • Google Slides / Keynote: Quick and functional if not beautiful

Different One-sheet Versions to Maintain

BOOKING ONE-SHEET: Emphasises live performance history, technical requirements summary, set lengths, and booking contact.

PRESS ONE-SHEET: Emphasises music, bio, press quotes, and streaming links. Less focus on technical requirements.

BRAND / SPONSORSHIP ONE-SHEET: Emphasises audience demographics, engagement rates, brand alignment, and social reach. Includes information a brand manager needs: audience age/gender/city breakdown (from Instagram Insights), engagement rate, and examples of previous brand partnerships.

SYNC ONE-SHEET: Emphasises music genres and moods, previous sync placements, rights ownership, and music supervisor contact information.

Album Release Plan Timeline

A free, practical guide for independent Indian artists. Part of the Indie Music India toolkit.

A week-by-week countdown timeline for a full album release campaign in India. Adjust for EP or single releases by scaling the timeline proportionally.

12 Weeks Before Release

  • Album budget finalised and approved (see Album Budget Template)
  • All tracks recorded, mixed, and mastered — master files received
  • ISRC codes confirmed for all tracks
  • UPC code confirmed for album
  • Co-writer split sheets signed for all tracks
  • IPRS registrations filed for all compositions
  • PPL India registrations filed for all recordings
  • Cover artwork finalised and delivered
  • Decide release strategy: all at once / staggered singles leading to album
  • Determine lead single(s) for pre-release campaign
  • Artist bio updated and approved

10 Weeks Before Release

  • Upload to distributor (DistroKid / TuneCore / Believe / OKListen)
  • Metadata entered and verified
  • Release date set in distributor platform
  • Pre-save link generated (Spotify, Apple Music)
  • Pre-order activated (if physical product included)
  • PR agency / publicist briefed and activated
  • Press release first draft written
  • Media list compiled: Wild City, Homegrown, Rolling Stone India, The Hindu, Scroll.in, city-specific press, relevant blogs and podcasts
  • Music video for lead single in final editing (if applicable)
  • Lyric video / visualiser in production (if applicable)

8 Weeks Before Release

  • Lead single released (if staggered campaign)
  • Spotify editorial pitch submitted for lead single (minimum 7 days before — do this now for 8 weeks if submitting early for a single releasing in 7 days)
  • Apple Music for Artists pitch submitted
  • JioSaavn editorial pitch (via distributor or direct contact)
  • Press release finalised
  • Long-lead press outreach begins (magazines, long-format features)
  • Album announcement post live on social media
  • Pre-save campaign launched
  • Email/WhatsApp list announcement: album coming
  • Announce release date on all social platforms

6 Weeks Before Release

  • Short-lead press outreach (news outlets, online music blogs, influencers)
  • Social media content calendar live (6 weeks of daily/near-daily posts planned)
  • Behind-the-scenes content released (studio photos, making-of videos)
  • Music video for lead single released (if applicable)
  • Interview bookings confirmed: podcast appearances, video interviews, editorial
  • Radio plugger engaged (Radio Mirchi, Big FM, Red FM, Radio City, Fever FM, AIR)
  • Physical product manufacturing confirmed (if applicable)
  • Release show / launch event booked (venue, date, ticket platform: Insider.in / BookMyShow / Paytm Insider)
  • Second single announced (if staggered)

4 Weeks Before Release

  • Second single released (if staggered campaign)
  • Spotify editorial pitch for album submitted (full album pitch — not a second single pitch; use the album pitch section in Spotify for Artists)
  • Album trailer / visualiser released on YouTube/Instagram
  • Interviews going live: 2–3 minimum in this window
  • Countdowns on Instagram Stories / YouTube Community tab
  • Launch show ticket sales open
  • Playlist pitching campaign to independent curators (Spotify / YouTube / JioSaavn)
  • Retargeting ad campaign set up (Facebook / Instagram ads targeting past listeners)
  • Physical copies (if any) shipped from manufacturer to artist

2 Weeks Before Release

  • All pre-release press embargoes set (confirm with each outlet)
  • Press album stream links sent to confirmed reviewers
  • Third single / b-side / acoustic version released (if applicable)
  • Full track listing revealed on social media
  • Radio airplay beginning (check with plugger)
  • Email/WhatsApp campaign: 2 weeks to release reminder
  • Launch show promotion at peak intensity
  • Physical copies received and ready for dispatch
  • Pre-orders / pre-saves at maximum promotional push

Release Week

Monday: "3 days to go" content Tuesday: Final preview content / listening snippets Wednesday (or release day): ALBUM LIVE

  • Check all platforms: Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk, YouTube Music, Amazon Music
  • All social posts live (Instagram post, Reel, Stories, YouTube community, Facebook, Twitter/X)
  • Email/WhatsApp announcement sent
  • Press coverage goes live (coordinated with embargo)
  • Music video live (YouTube premiere or standard post)
  • Artist active on social all day: responding, sharing, thanking

Post-release day:

  • Fan UGC (user-generated content) shared and celebrated
  • Monitor streaming analytics daily
  • Respond to all press coverage
  • Continue promotional interviews

Weeks 1–4 Post-release

  • Monitor Spotify for Artists and Chartmetric daily
  • Share milestone posts (1,000 streams, 10,000 streams, etc.)
  • Continue playlist pitching
  • Live session / acoustic performance released on YouTube
  • Launch show held
  • Fan contest or interactive campaign (optional)
  • Review tour dates or additional shows in response to demand data

Weeks 4–12 Post-release

  • Monthly streaming report reviewed
  • IPRS royalty accumulation tracked
  • Planning for next cycle begins
  • Physical merch bundle campaign (if stock remains)
  • Potential remix or collaboration release to extend album's life
  • Year-end lists outreach (if releasing in Q4)

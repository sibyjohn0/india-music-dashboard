Run Sheet Template

A free, ready-to-use template for independent Indian artists. Read it here, or make your own copy to fill in.

A minute-by-minute schedule for show day. The run sheet is the single most important operational document on the day of a show. Everyone, artist, crew, venue staff, promoter, should have a copy.

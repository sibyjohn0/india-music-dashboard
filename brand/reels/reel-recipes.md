# Indie Music India — 10 Reel templates: animation recipes + Veo prompts

HOW TO USE EACH
1. In Canva, new 1080x1920 design. Upload the template PNG as the background/base.
2. Recreate the text as editable text layers on top (fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Inter,
   Space Mono) so you can retype per topic. Keep everything in the same safe band:
   nothing above y=280px or below y=1560px; side margins 130px.
3. Drop your Veo clip (or screen-record / talking-head) BEHIND the text where the
   template leaves empty space.
4. Apply the animations below (Canva > select element > Animate).
5. Export MP4 silent. Add trending audio + captions inside Instagram.

Global page animation: set Page Animation = "Rise" or "Fade" (subtle). Avoid gimmicks.
Keep total 12-22s. One beat on screen at a time.

------------------------------------------------------------------
01 NUMBER REVEAL (Receipts) — dark, giant gradient number
Use for: a stat/payout/figure. Structure: setup line -> BIG number -> caveat.
Animate: eyebrow Fade (0.0s) · setup line Rise (0.3s) · the big number POP or
"Baseline" (1.2s, after a beat of suspense) · caveat Fade (1.8s) · handle Fade.
Veo [SCENE]: macro of glowing rupee coins and rising light bars, streams of light
flowing into a small pile of coins, shallow depth of field.

02 MYTH-BUSTER (Receipts) — cream, strike-through then reveal
Use for: correcting a common belief. Structure: the myth (struck out) -> "Actually" -> truth.
Animate: myth text Rise, then the strike line "Wipe"/"Ascend" across it (0.8s) ·
"Actually ->" Fade (1.4s) · truth Rise (1.7s).
Veo [SCENE]: a red line drawing itself across a clean surface, then dissolving into
soft brand-gradient light, minimal, elegant.

03 HOW-TO STEPS (Receipts) — cream, numbered chips
Use for: any process. Structure: title -> steps 1..4 appear one by one.
Animate: title Rise · each numbered row "Ascend" or Fade, staggered 0.4s apart ·
handle Fade. (Time each step to your voiceover / on-screen read.)
Veo [SCENE]: OR skip AI — this reads best as talking-head or a plain brand background.

04 COMPARISON (Receipts) — split screen VS
Use for: X vs Y. Structure: left option, right option, VS badge.
Animate: left column slides in from left ("Pan") · right from right · VS badge POP/"Breathe" ·
handle Fade.
Veo [SCENE]: OR use a clean split of two textures; talking-head works too.

05 LIST / 3 THINGS (Receipts) — cream rows
Use for: "3 / 5 things" lists. Structure: title -> rows with accent dots.
Animate: title Rise · rows Fade in staggered 0.35s · handle Fade.
Veo [SCENE]: soft floating brand-color orbs on cream, slow drift, lots of space.

06 POV / RELATABLE (Real Talk) — gradient + subtitle band
Use for: relatable/meme moments. Structure: subtitle-style caption over a scene.
Animate: caption band "Typewriter" or Rise · keep it snappy · handle Fade.
Veo [SCENE]: an artist checking their phone alone in a warmly lit room at night,
candid, cinematic, a little wistful, clean space up top.

07 MOTIVATION (Real Talk) — gradient, big statement
Use for: a truth / mantra. Structure: one bold line -> supporting line.
Animate: statement "Baseline"/Rise word-by-word feel · supporting line Fade (1.2s).
Veo [SCENE]: an intimate live gig, small crowd lit by stage color, hands up, shot
from the stage, cinematic, slow motion.

08 QUICK TIP (Receipts) — yellow
Use for: one sharp tip. Structure: tip headline -> one-line why.
Animate: eyebrow Fade · tip Rise · why line Fade (0.8s) · handle Fade. Keep under 12s.
Veo [SCENE]: OR none — solid yellow brand background is enough; add a subtle grain.

09 NEWS / SIGNAL (Signal) — dark
Use for: the sourced news take. Structure: headline -> "what it means" -> source line.
Animate: eyebrow Fade · headline Rise · "what it means" Fade · source line Fade.
ALWAYS show the source. Post the considered take, not the hot take.
Veo [SCENE]: abstract flowing gradient ribbons in brand colors, clean, editorial.

10 TOOL DEMO / SCREEN-RECORD (Receipts) — cream, device frame
Use for: showing the calculator or Spotify for Artists. Structure: question -> screen.
Animate: question Rise · the screen-recording plays inside the device frame ·
handle Fade. (No Veo — screen-record the real tool with sliders moving.)

MASTER VEO PROMPT (swap [SCENE]):
Create a vertical 9:16 video, 8 seconds, 1080x1920. Scene: [SCENE]. Modern editorial
music-brand aesthetic, bright candy-pop but premium and tasteful. Color grade around
pink #FF4D8D, violet #8B5CF6, blue #3AA0FF, warm cream #FFF7EE, small pops of yellow
#FFD23F. India independent-music context. Soft cinematic lighting, shallow depth of
field, smooth slow push-in or parallax, high dynamic range, no flicker. Keep the top
third and bottom third as clean empty negative space for text overlays. No on-screen
text, no captions, no logos, no watermark. Confident calm mood. Loop cleanly. Audio:
subtle modern indie-electronic bed, no vocals.

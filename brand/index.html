Foundation

What this is

Indie Music India gives independent Indian musicians the things a label usually provides, minus the label. Free, data-driven tools and guides for the whole scene, plus a hands-on paid Artist Development Programme for a small number of artists. The through-line: everything around the music — the economics, the identity, the network, and the release.

Who it's for

Independent artists in India across languages and genres, and the people around them: managers, labels, curators, brands.

What we sound like

A knowledgeable insider who is honest, specific, and warm. Never a hype man, never a guru. We show receipts.

Why it works

Credibility from reliable sources and India-specific truth AI can't cheaply copy. We help through free and paid resources, in that order.

The programme, in one line. Siby John and Karishma Changroth work directly with a few independent artists at a time on release strategy, identity, the money, and the network, backed by a bench of specialists (PR, social, marketing, video, design, legal, IPRS) brought in per release. 01
Voice & tone

How we speak

Principles.

  • Clear over clever. Plain language an artist actually uses.
  • Specific over generic. Real names, real numbers, real steps.
  • India-first. Rupees, IPRS, JioSaavn, local venues.
  • Sourced. Every factual claim traces to a named source; date evergreen numbers.
  • Honest. Ranges not false precision.
  • Qualitative for performance. "One of the top-reaching posts," not raw dashboards.

Three content lanes. Every post is one of these.

Receipts — sourced data and how-to. Saves and authority.
Real Talk — classy, motivating, insider meme. Feeling seen, not slapstick.
Signal — the sourced, considered take on news, not the hot take.

Say this, not that

money in vs money back~~unit economics~~
how listeners find you / pass you on~~the algorithm / reach signals~~
roughly / a range / an estimate~~a single exact number~~
reviewers, curators, blogs~~influencers~~
a comma, a colon, or a rewrite~~an em dash~~
Hard rules. No em dashes. Never "the algorithm," "engagement velocity," "distribution cycle," or hype words ("game-changer," "explode," "secret hack"). One idea per post. Must be save-worthy.

Color

The poppy palette

A candy-bright, high-energy family on warm cream or deep ink. Tap any swatch to copy the hex.

Signature gradients

Grape · orange → pink → violet
Mint · mint → blue → violet

Usage

  • Cream = default light ground. Ink = dark / "guide" ground.
  • Accents color labels, dots, highlights, one tile per layout.
  • Gradients for heroes, CTA slides, video beds. Diagonal, three stops.

Contrast

  • Ink text on cream/yellow/mint. Cream text on ink/violet/blue/pink.
  • Never yellow-on-cream or ink-on-violet.
  • Text over gradient/photo needs a scrim or shadow.

Visual language

The building blocks

  • 2px ink borders + a hard 6px offset shadow (no blur) on tiles and cards.
  • Colour-blocked tiles: one flat accent or gradient per tile, high-contrast type.
  • Corner circles: a solid accent circle off the top-right, a second bottom-left. Never over the text.
  • Eyebrow labels in Space Mono, accent-colored, above every headline.
  • "SWIPE →" on carousel covers; "link in bio ↓" only on promo, never educational.

Photography & imagery

  • Real over stock: independent artists, home studios, small venues, rehearsal rooms, the actual Indian scene.
  • Warm natural light, candid not corporate. Feature real faces, including the founders.
  • As backgrounds: duotone or gradient-overlay in brand colors, always a scrim before text.
  • Avoid: cheesy "musician-with-headphones" stock, generic laser concerts, fake AI faces, Western-only imagery.

Grid rhythm

  • Alternate bright promo tiles and dark guide covers so the grid reads as two content types.
  • Break up rows with an artist feature or a gradient post.
  • Never three near-identical tiles in a row.

09
Social strategy

How we grow

Model: the website disseminated into Instagram. Site = library, IG = distribution. Reels drive reach and follows; carousels drive saves and authority. Growth is Reels-led.

Weekly cadence

Reels ×5The growth engine. Non-follower reach → follows.
Carousels ×2Saves, shares, search. Authority layer.
Stories dailyWarmth, polls, reposts, features.
Feature ×1An artist / scene peer. Reach + goodwill.

Atomize the site

  • Each guide → 1 carousel + 2–3 Reels.
  • Each answer → one 15s Reel.
  • Radar / reviewer data → "receipts" Reels (real numbers, sourced).
  • News → a sourced Signal carousel + talking-head Reel.

Caption formula

Line 1 = the exact search phrase. Then the value. Close with "Save this," not "buy." Then 4–6 tight hashtags. Educational captions carry no "link in bio." Set alt text = the headline.

Discovery, not a hard sell

90% playground, in the scene, useful. Never pointed straight at the programme; a few passes to the goal is fine. But never a dead end: bio, a pinned post, and the tools are always one tap away.

EXAMPLE · RECEIPTS

"How much does Spotify pay per stream in India? Not a fixed rate, a share of a local revenue pool. Roughly ₹0.03–0.05. Save this."

EXAMPLE · REAL TALK

"POV: you released the song, then remembered you never pitched a single playlist. The pitch happens 4 weeks before."

EXAMPLE · SIGNAL

"[This week's India music news] and what it actually means for independent artists. The considered take, with sources."

10
Content guidelines

What we make, and how we check it

The sourcing standard

  • Every factual claim names a verifiable source (Spotify Loud & Clear, IPRS.org, copyright.gov.in, PPL India, distributor T&Cs, Luminate/IFPI, credible reporting).
  • Numbers get a range, not false precision, and a "last verified" date on evergreen figures.
  • Fact-check gate before anything factual ships. One wrong number costs us more than anyone.
  • AI can draft; a human verifies every stat and India-specific claim.

What earns a post

  • Save-worthy or it doesn't go. A checklist, a comparison, a real number, a genuine laugh.
  • India-specific > generic. If a chatbot can answer it blandly, add the India truth, the source, or the POV.
  • One idea per post. One clear takeaway.
  • Show, don't claim. Feature real artists, real data, real faces.
Locked fact — Spotify India payout (verified Aug 2026). ~₹0.03–0.05 per stream → 1,000 India streams ≈ ₹30–50, a million ≈ ₹30,000–50,000. International ≈ ₹0.25–0.42 (global $0.003–0.005). Sources: Ditto, TuneCore, Chartlex, India reporting. Use this everywhere. Never quote a per-stream number that contradicts it.

11
Guardrails

Do & Don't

✓Do

  • Design at 3:4 (1080×1440) with 165px side safe margins.
  • Lead captions with the exact search phrase; end educational posts with "Save this."
  • Cite a source and date evergreen numbers.
  • Use one accent or gradient per tile; keep type high-contrast.
  • Keep memes classy and scene-smart.
  • Feature real artists and real, sourced data.
  • Overlay brand text yourself; use AI for visuals only.
  • Add trending audio inside the Instagram app.

✕Don't

  • Post 1:1 or 4:5, they crop in the grid.
  • Use em dashes, "the algorithm," or hype words.
  • Say "unit economics" or jargon aimed at artists.
  • Quote a per-stream number that contradicts the locked figure.
  • Let AI render the logo, handle, or headline text.
  • Put "link in bio" on educational content.
  • Take a brand deal that would soften an honest comparison.
  • Chase follower count over saves, shares, and DMs.

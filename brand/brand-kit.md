## 00 Foundation
Free tools + guides + a paid Artist Development Programme for independent Indian musicians.
Theme: **everything around the music** (economics, identity, network, release).
Audience: indie artists in India + the people around them (managers, labels, curators, brands).

## 01 Voice
Clear over clever · specific (real names/numbers/steps) · India-first (rupees, IPRS, JioSaavn) · sourced (name a source, date evergreen numbers, ranges not false precision) · honest · warm · premium not hypey · qualitative for performance.
**Hard rules:** no em dashes (comma/colon/rewrite); never "the algorithm", "engagement velocity", "distribution cycle", or hype words; never "unit economics" (say "money in vs money back").
**Lanes:** Receipts (sourced data/how-to) · Real Talk (classy motivating / insider meme) · Signal (sourced considered news take, not the hot take).

## 06 Image spec
- **1080×1440 (3:4)** — matches IG profile-grid preview.
- **165px side safe margins** (content in central ~750px) — survives grid, feed, and the 9:16 boost/story crop. (1:1 loses ~12.5%/side, 4:5 ~3%; only 3:4 is clean.)
- Headline Bricolage 64–96px (auto-fit, max 3 lines) · body Inter ~40px · labels Space Mono ~30px.
- Footer bottom-left: ◉ @indiemusicindia.co, ~120px from bottom.
- Carousel: dark cover (kicker + headline + INSIDE list + SWIPE) → cream teaching slides (LABEL · counter · title · body · handle) → gradient CTA close.

## 08 Social strategy
Model: the website disseminated into Instagram. Site = library, IG = distribution. Reels drive reach/follows; carousels drive saves/authority. Growth is Reels-led.
Weekly: **5 Reels + 2 carousels + daily Stories + 1 artist feature.** Batch production.
Atomize: guide→1 carousel + 2–3 Reels; answer→one 15s Reel; data→"receipts" Reels; news→Signal carousel + talking-head.
Caption formula: line 1 = exact search phrase; then value; close "Save this" (educational) or soft CTA (promo); 4–6 tight hashtags; alt text = headline. Educational captions carry no "link in bio".
Discovery: 90% value, indirect funnel, never a hard sell and never a dead end (bio/pinned/tools always one tap away). Optimize saves/shares/DMs, not follower count.

## 09 Content guidelines
Sourcing standard: every factual claim names a verifiable source; ranges not false precision; "last verified" date on evergreen numbers; fact-check gate before anything factual ships; AI drafts, human verifies every stat and India claim.
What earns a post: save-worthy (checklist/comparison/real number/genuine laugh); India-specific > generic; one idea per post; show don't claim.

## 10 Do & Don't
**Do:** design 3:4 with 165px margins · lead captions with the search phrase, close educational with "Save this" · cite a source, date evergreen numbers · one accent/gradient per tile, high-contrast type · classy scene-smart memes · feature real artists + sourced data · overlay brand text yourself (AI = visuals only) · add trending audio in-app.
**Don't:** post 1:1 or 4:5 · use em dashes / "the algorithm" / hype words · say "unit economics" · quote a per-stream number that contradicts the locked figure · let AI render the logo/handle/headline · put "link in bio" on educational content · take a brand deal that softens an honest comparison · chase follower count over saves/shares/DMs.

DGTL India 2026: Bengaluru & Goa, November 13–15

The international electronic festival returns for its sixth Indian edition, with two city experiences back to back, and a first-ever landing in Goa.

DGTL is one of the few global electronic brands that has built a real, repeat presence in India rather than a one-off import. This year it runs two experiences on consecutive weekends-into-weekend: a Bengaluru edition, then its debut in Goa. Both carry DGTL's signature stages, the Modular and the Generator by BudX, and the audiovisual production the brand is known for.

The stages

DGTL runs its two signature stages, the Modular and the Generator by BudX, with the immersive production and programming approach the festival carries across its global editions. It is a curated electronic experience rather than a multi-genre festival, which is what sets it apart from the larger India festival calendar.

Who's behind it

DGTL India is organised by Forward India, led by founder Arnab Banerjee, now six editions into building the brand in the country.
